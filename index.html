Questions we get from serious buyers

Is this another AI content generator?
No. Generation is the last step. The system's work is analysis, scoring and decision-making — every recommendation ships with its reasoning and a confidence range.
How do you handle data governance?
Workspace isolation, tiered governance roles, a full audit trail in the Transparency Engine, and safety filtering on every model output.
How long until it's useful?
Onboarding builds the Brand Brain in one session. Forecasts start at ±40% error and tighten to about ±10% as your own outcome data accumulates.
Does it replace our stack?
It sits above it. Ads platforms, CRM, analytics and WhatsApp stay where they are; the intelligence layer reads them and writes decisions back.
